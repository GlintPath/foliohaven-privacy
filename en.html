Foliohaven Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 20, 2026

中文 | Support

1. What We Collect

Foliohaven stores your order data only on your local device. We do not run an account system on any server — there is no "sign-in".

2. iCloud Sync (Optional)

If you enable iCloud sync (a Plus feature), Foliohaven uses Apple's iCloud service to sync your order data — encrypted — to your own iCloud account. We cannot see this data, and Apple does not share it with us.

3. CloudKit Public Database

Foliohaven uses Apple's CloudKit Public Database only to count Founder Edition numbers:

  • When you purchase Plus, we write one record containing: transaction ID, product ID, region code (e.g. "US"), purchase timestamp, and a one-way hash of your device identifier
  • We do not write: your name, email, or any order data
  • This data is used solely to compute "you are the Nth Founder globally / Mth in your region"

4. Third Parties

Foliohaven does not integrate any third-party SDK, and we do not share data with third parties.

5. Ads / Tracking

Foliohaven has no ads and does not track any of your behavior.

6. Data Deletion

Deleting the app removes all local data. iCloud-stored data can be deleted via iOS Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Storage → Foliohaven → Delete Data.
