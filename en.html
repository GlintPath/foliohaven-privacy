Privacy and your data

Last updated: November 5, 2026

简体中文 · 繁體中文 · English · Support

Local data and accounts

Orders, client names, tasks, amounts, costs, payments, refunds, notes, and service templates are stored on your device. You do not need a Foliohaven account. The app has no advertising SDK. RevenueCat processes purchase information as described below.

Optional iCloud sync

Apple handles optional Plus iCloud sync using your iCloud account; orders do not pass through a server operated by us. Sync choices apply on the next launch, and Settings shows the current mode. Sync is not an independent backup. Turning it off does not delete previously uploaded data.

Purchases and access

Apple processes payments. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app identifier, purchase receipts and subscription status to verify and restore Plus access and provide purchase analytics. Your ledger, client details, notes and PIN are not sent to RevenueCat. Foliohaven keeps the verified access status on this device. Apple retains payment-card details; neither Foliohaven nor RevenueCat receives them from the app. RevenueCat uses its own infrastructure to process this purchase information, which may be outside your country. We do not send an email address, advertising identifier, or personal account identifier to RevenueCat. To request help accessing or deleting purchase-service data, contact jeff@fortupath.com. Deleting local ledger data or uninstalling the app does not delete Apple or RevenueCat transaction records or cancel a subscription. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

App lock and PIN

PIN verification information is kept in the device Keychain. The system handles biometrics; the app does not obtain face or fingerprint data. PIN recovery requires system device-owner authentication. App lock does not encrypt exported files.

Notifications, widgets, and Siri

With permission, notifications and system widgets may show order labels, amounts, or dates. App lock hides sensitive system content and blocks Siri access to the ledger. The Siri setting controls these features; current suggestions do not include client names or order content.

Backups and sharing

Full JSON backups and CSV exports contain business information, including orders and private notes. They are not additionally encrypted, so choose their location and recipients carefully. Receipt images exclude internal costs, project net income, and private notes by default; you can enable an internal version in the preview. Backups exclude the PIN, purchase credentials, and system permissions.

Deletion and restoration

Data and reset can delete orders, ledger entries, and service templates. When sync is active in the current session, deletions also reach other devices. Resetting preferences is separate. Files saved or sent elsewhere must be managed separately. Uninstalling the app does not revoke purchases or confirm cloud or external-file deletion. Verify a restorable backup before deleting records.

Feedback and contact

Feedback emails you choose to send include your message, app version, and build number. You can edit them in your mail app before sending. We receive the email and sender address to respond to your feedback. The app does not automatically attach orders, the PIN, or logs. Contact: jeff@fortupath.com.
